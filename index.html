Butter Queries Answered

What types of peanut butter does Mamamade Peanuts offer?

Mamamade Peanuts offers a variety of peanut butter including smooth, crunchy, and flavored options to suit diverse customer preferences.

Can I place an order from outside Nairobi?

Yes, we deliver nationwide across Kenya. Orders can be placed online and will be shipped to your location.

How do I store my peanut butter for maximum freshness?

Store unopened jars in a cool, dry place. Once opened, refrigeration is recommended to maintain freshness and extend shelf life.

Are Mamamade Peanut products allergen-free?

Our products are made with peanuts which are a known allergen. We do not recommend them for individuals with peanut allergies.

What payment methods are accepted for online orders?

We accept multiple payment methods including mobile money transfers, credit/debit cards, and bank transfers for your convenience.
